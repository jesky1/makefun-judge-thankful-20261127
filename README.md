## The entry

> You have read a thousand thank-yous today, and not one of them was for you. So here is one that is — with nothing attached. No plea, no pitch. Only a bench under the big tree at the end of this paragraph, with your name carved small, like someone knew you would come. Sit a minute. The queue will wait. Thank you for staying.

One paragraph, 325 characters, picked from twelve drafts: it thanks the judge
for the work itself, asks for nothing, and ends on a concrete image instead of
a compliment.
